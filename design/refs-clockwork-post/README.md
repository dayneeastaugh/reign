## Reusable treatment references

- `08_ordinary_stamp_stop_reference.png` — lit/done ordinary stop, isolated from its rating rail
- `09_parcel_stop_reference.png` — lit/done parcel stop, isolated from its rating rail
- `10_sorting_hall_stop_reference.png` — lit/done sorting-hall press, isolated from its rating rail
- `11_room_fascia_floor_slab_treatment.png` — full-width storey divider and adjoining room treatment
- `12_pneumatic_tube_coupling_treatment.png` — brass tube, bends, couplings, level discs, and parcel junction
- `13_star_rating_rail.png` — completed three-star rail

The small reusable crops are visual references for procedural recreation, not production sprites. Their dark backgrounds are retained because glow, contrast, and edge treatment are part of the implementation reference.
